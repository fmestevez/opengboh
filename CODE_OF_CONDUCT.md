# Code of conduct

opengboh is a hobby project made by people who like these old wargames.
We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions or
anywhere else the project is represented) to be able to do so without
harassment, whatever their age, background, body, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people whose
  first language isn't English.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with arguments and evidence;
  accept that the maintainers make the final call.
- Give and take criticism of code and ideas gracefully, and own up to your
  mistakes.
- Respect the project's legal ground rules (see
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)): don't post the games' data or
  decompiled code, and don't help others pirate the games.

## What isn't acceptable

- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling, and deliberately
  derailing discussions.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwanted
  sexual attention and sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a
  welcoming community.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers by opening a confidential report on
GitHub or by contacting a maintainer directly (see the repository owner's
profile). Reports are handled promptly and privately.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban
anyone whose behaviour is harmful, depending on how serious it is and
whether it keeps happening.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
